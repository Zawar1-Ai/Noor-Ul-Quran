الإِسْلَام

About Islam

Islam means peaceful submission to Allah — the One God, Creator of the heavens and the earth.

Belief

Muslims believe in one God (Allah), His angels, His revealed books, His messengers, the Last Day, and divine decree. Prophet Muhammad ﷺ is the final messenger, and the Quran is the final revelation.

Way of life

Islam teaches prayer, honesty, kindness to parents, care for the poor, justice, and good character. It invites every person to remember Allah in daily life and to treat creation with mercy.

Six articles of faith

Allah

Belief in the oneness of God, without partner or equal.

Angels

Created from light, they carry out Allah’s commands.

Books

Revelations including the Torah, Gospel, Psalms, and the Quran.

Messengers

From Adam to Muhammad ﷺ, peace be upon them all.

Last Day

Resurrection, accountability, Paradise and Hellfire.

Qadr

Divine decree — Allah’s knowledge and will over all things.

99 Names of Allah

A selection of the Beautiful Names for reflection.
